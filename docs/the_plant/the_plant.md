# The Plant 🌱

- **Pothos Plant** (Epipremnum Aureum)
- Sitting indoor, in Nordhavn (Copenhagen), 5th floor, in a corner, with not too much direct sun light (only some hours depending of the season)
- Around 5 to 6 years old
- Large plant having around 8 to 10 branches, adding more than 40 meters
- Pot:
    - Terracotta 23cm (diameter) x 20cm (high) with drainage hole
- Soil:
    - Champost Pottemuld (~75%) with Perlite (~25%)
- Fertilizer:
    - Universal Fertilizer, 100% organic (3% N, 1% P, 5 %K): every 2 to 3 months
    - Mixed with water, 10ml of fertilizer per 2 liter of water, approximately
