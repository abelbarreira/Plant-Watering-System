## About RS PRO Vertical Vs. BBC micro:bit V2 controller

Refer to [RS PRO_vertical](<04_float_switch/RS PRO_vertical.pdf>)
    https://dk.rs-online.com/web/p/niveausensorer-niveauswitche/0307103

The micro:bit V2 powers the circuit using its internal 3.3 V pull-up resistor. The float switch itself needs no separate power supply.

P0
    Float switch — wire 1
    Digital input
GND
    Float switch — wire 2
    Ground connection

The two switch wires are interchangeable. Do not connect the switch to 3V or to an external voltage source.
The micro:bit itself still needs its normal USB or battery power supply.
