## Real Design

- A real IRLZ44N on a small pump is normally fine at 3.3 V, but verify with the drain-to-source voltage test.
- For the real build: add a fuse of about 1–2 A on the 12 V line, and confirm that the pump’s voltage rating matches the supply.
- Capacitors
    - C1 (100 nF) capacitor ceramic rated for 50v:
        - The value and the position are correct. It is a high-frequency noise filter for the brushes, and it belongs directly across the motor’s terminals. Use a ceramic part rated for 50 V. It does not help with startup.
    - C2 (220 µF) capacitor electrolytic rated for, at least 25v:
        - Across +12 V and GND, as close as possible to the MOSFET and the motor connector, with short leads and the + leg on +12 V. Use at least a 25 V rating. It supplies the motor’s startup current locally, so the 12 V rail doesn’t dip and glitch the micro:bit or the sensor.
